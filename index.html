About Me

Hi, I’m Hafizullah Sarwary, and I'm a passionate Geoinformatics Engineer currently completing my Master's at Politecnico di Milano. My real-world foundation comes from spending over six years tackling infrastructure challenges at DABS, where I specialized in making huge transmission projects happen—everything from topographic surveys and route planning to creating detailed GIS maps and running terrain analysis. Now, my focus is on the cutting edge of research. My Master's projects at POLIMI have been all about applying advanced techniques: visualizing air quality, building flood risk models, analyzing urbanization trends, and even using machine learning with satellite imagery for soil classification. Ultimately, I'm driven to connect GIS, Remote Sensing, and Data Science to deliver tangible, sustainable solutions for the future of urban life, energy planning, and environmental resilience.
